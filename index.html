🧠 Smart Money Tips

Simple habits that work in real life — especially when social media makes everything feel urgent.

⏰
Use the 24-Hour Rule

Wait one day before buying an unplanned item. The urge may become smaller.

📝
Track Every Purchase

Small purchases add up. Recording them helps you see where your money really goes.

🎯
Needs Before Wants

Handle important expenses first, then decide what you can spend for fun.

💳
Set a Spending Limit

Give yourself a weekly or monthly shopping limit before scrolling.

🔕
Reduce Buying Pressure

Mute accounts that constantly push products you don't actually need.

🤔
Ask One Question

“Would I still want this if I had never seen it on social media?”

⭐ Most realistic habit

For Indonesian teenagers, a practical starting point is tracking spending + the 24-hour rule. Both are free, easy to do with a phone, and don't require advanced financial knowledge. Together, they help you see where your money goes and create a pause before FOMO turns into a purchase.
